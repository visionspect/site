Three ways we help

Whether the camera is already on the line or still an idea, here's where we come in.

Vision System Health Check

“Is my system still catching what it should?”

Lighting ages, parts change, someone loosened a threshold to stop the false rejects. We test your system against known good and bad parts and show you where it stands.

  • Detection test with real defects
  • Statistics on how repeatable it is
  • Check against your quality standards
  • Written report and fix plan

New Inspection

“What's the right system for this inspection?”

Short shots, missing features, labels, codes, dimensions. We scope it, pick the hardware, integrate it, and prove it works on your line before we call it done.

  • Feasibility test on your actual parts
  • Camera, lens and lighting selection
  • PLC and line integration
  • Validation with documented results

Support & Tuning

“Who keeps it running after install?”

New part revision, new supplier, false rejects creeping up. We retrain, troubleshoot and check in on a schedule so your operators stop bypassing the camera.

  • Retraining for part and process changes
  • Troubleshooting, on site or remote
  • Scheduled check-ups
  • Operator and technician training

We pick the system that fits your part. Not the one we're paid to sell.

Most integrators are a sales channel for one camera brand. We're not tied to hardware sales, so the recommendation starts with your part, your line speed and your budget.

We work across Cognex, Keyence and other platforms, and we'll integrate with equipment you already own when it can do the job.

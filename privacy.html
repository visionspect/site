Privacy

Last updated November 1, 2026. This page covers visionspect.com, run by VisionSpect Solutions LLC in Greenville, South Carolina.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, company, work email, phone number if you give one, and what you tell us about your inspection. If you arrived from a tagged link (for example a QR code on a flyer), the form also records that tag so we know which channel brought you here.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you and to follow up on the work you asked about. We don't sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored by Netlify, which hosts this site, and are forwarded to nick@visionspect.com. Email is handled through Google Workspace.

Visit counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across other sites. It tells us things like how many people viewed a page and which country they came from.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete what you've sent us, email nick@visionspect.com.
